Powered by an ESP32-S3 edge controller, 4G LTE connectivity, and an n8n cloud automation engine, the VoltGo smart solar street pole manages clean energy, EV and mobile charging, and public safety in four seamless steps:

Precision Radar Tracking & Adaptive Lighting (RD-03D)
The RD-03D 24GHz multi-target millimeter-wave radar tracks approaching pedestrians and vehicles with high accuracy, smoothly ramping street lighting from 20% eco-standby to 100% brightness while waking up the 7-inch interactive HMI touchscreen.

Instant RFID / QR Scan & n8n Verification
Users tap a registered RFID card or scan the on-screen QR code. The pole dispatches a real-time request over 4G LTE to our n8n workflow engine, which instantly validates user credentials and balance against the cloud database.

Intelligent EV & Multi-Device Charging
Once n8n authorizes the session, the controller safely engages the isolated 220V AC contactor for EV charging, alongside QC3.0 / PD3.0 wired fast-charging ports and a 15W Qi wireless pad—continuously balanced by real-time solar and battery monitoring.

Automated n8n Cloud Logging & Emergency SOS
Solar generation, battery health, and charging logs are routed 24/7 through n8n to cloud dashboards. In an emergency, pressing the vandal-proof SOS panic button captures a live snapshot (OV2640 camera) and triggers an automated n8n alert directly to Telegram.
