How VoltGo Works

A smart solar street pole combining adaptive lighting, real 220V EV charging, and cloud automation in four simple steps.

01

Radar Detection & Lighting

A 24GHz radar tracks approaching pedestrians and vehicles, ramping street lights from 20% to 100% and waking up the touchscreen.

24GHz mmWave Radar Adaptive Dimming
02

RFID & QR Authentication

Users tap an RFID card or scan the QR code. The pole instantly verifies credentials and balance through cloud automation over 4G LTE.

RFID / QR Scan n8n Cloud Verification
03

220V EV & Phone Charging

Once approved, an isolated 220V contactor safely powers the EV charging cable, alongside QC/PD fast USB ports and a 15W wireless pad.

220V AC Contactor Fast USB & 15W Qi
04

Live Telemetry & SOS Safety

Energy and battery telemetry are logged in real time. In an emergency, the panic SOS button snaps a photo and alerts Telegram immediately.

Camera Snapshot Telegram SOS Alert
